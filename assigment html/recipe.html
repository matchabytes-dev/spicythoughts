How to Make Seblak 🌶️

Ingredients

  • 100 g crackers
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 3 shallots
  • 3 red chilies
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 spring onion
  • Water
  • Salt and seasoning
  • Cooking oil

How to Make

  1. Soak the crackers in warm water until they become soft.
  2. Grind the garlic, shallots, and red chilies into a paste.
  3. Heat some cooking oil and sauté the spice paste until fragrant.
  4. Add the eggs and scramble them together with the spices.
  5. Add water and bring it to a boil.
  6. Add the softened crackers and mix well.
  7. Add salt and seasoning according to your taste.
  8. Add spring onion and other toppings if desired.
  9. Cook for a few minutes and serve while warm.
